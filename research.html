Research

Research Contributions

  • Goal-oriented and semantic communications. Significance-driven, spectrum-efficient semantic transmission for mission-critical monitoring, the subject of two U.S. provisional patent applications [PA1, PA2]. SafeStep and MetaVIB-Air, a browser-based platform and a Software-Defined Radio (SDR) testbed that demonstrate semantic communication for pedestrian safety monitoring by combining computer vision on live traffic-camera feeds with a semantic transceiver based on the Variational Information Bottleneck (VIB), developed as co-first author [C2, P2].
  • Distributed online learning for network adaptation and timeliness. Fully distributed learning frameworks in which each node decides from local feedback: multi-player bandits for modulation adaptation [J1, C7], a bilevel bandit that jointly adapts modulation, power, and the Age of Information (AoI)-aware feedback interval under limited feedback [C4], and energy-fair bandit-based routing for multi-hop LoRa networks [C8]. Complementary lightweight bandit frameworks address multi-objective modulation adaptation, power control, OFDM carrier selection, and network cost-effectiveness on resource-constrained underwater nodes [J2, C5, C6, C9, C10], and learning-based routing provides resilience to jamming [J5]. Two extensions are under review: the bilevel bandit is reformulated as a partially observable Markov decision process with decision-dependent feedback delay and layerwise regret guarantees [U1], and OFDM carrier selection is generalized to joint subcarrier and power allocation through nested potential games [U2]. A survey of bandit methods for resource-constrained wireless devices, with a quantitative criterion of effective statelessness to decide when bandits can replace stateful reinforcement learning, is under review [U3].
  • Cross-layer optimization under covertness and mobility. The first distributed cross-layer algorithm for joint congestion control, routing, scheduling, and power control in covert multi-hop, multi-modal networks, built on a concave bound on the detection error probability and on a parallel proximal Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) with proven global linear convergence and robust tracking under fading and warden mobility, developed as co-first author [C1, P1]. A velocity-informed scheme adapts transmission parameters in high-mobility IoT scenarios [C3].
  • Stochastic channel modelling and prediction. Finite-state Markov and hidden Markov channel models and predictive estimators, including Kalman filtering, derived from real shallow-water acoustic traces of the LOON testbed of the NATO Centre for Maritime Research and Experimentation (CMRE) [J4, C11, P3], and a comparative study of predictive versus adaptive techniques in terms of accuracy and complexity [J3].
  • Experimental Internet of Underwater Things (IoUT) systems. An integrated acoustic/LoRa system for the transmission of multimedia sensor data over an IoUT, validated in nearshore shallow-water field trials [J6].

Research Projects

Participation as a researcher; sponsor and Principal Investigator (PI) are indicated where documented.

  • HEIDRA – Heterogeneous Edge Intelligence for Natural Disaster Response Analytics
    Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Auburn University, Auburn, AL, USA (Sep. 2026 – Present) Role: postdoctoral researcher Sponsor: U.S. National Science Foundation, Verticals-Enabling Intelligent Network Systems (VINES) Track 2 collaborative award. PI: Christopher G. Brinton (Purdue University). Co-PIs include Alexei Ashikhmin (Nokia Bell Labs), Shreyas Sundaram (Purdue University), Christopher Vo (Saab, Inc.), and Yin Sun (Auburn University). Partners: Purdue University, Auburn University, University of Virginia, Saab, Nokia. Resilient edge-intelligence platform for natural-disaster response that integrates mobile and multi-access edge computing with NextG wireless technologies to maintain reliable connectivity, enable rapid resource adaptation and reallocation, and provide on-site and on-device intelligence to emergency teams and first responders. The Auburn contribution develops importance-based, task-oriented networking that prioritizes mission-critical information over power- and capacity-limited disaster-zone networks.
  • Development of NextG Wireless Testbeds for Semantic and Goal-Oriented Communication
    Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Auburn University, Auburn, AL, USA (Jan. 2026 – Present) Role: postdoctoral researcher Sponsors: Alabama Research and Development Enhancement Fund (ARDEF), grant agreement 1ARDEF26 11; NVIDIA Academic Grant Program. PI: Yin Sun (Auburn University). Development and integration of end-to-end NextG testbeds for semantic and goal-oriented communication, with emphasis on protocol implementation, experimental validation, resource-constrained networking, and mission-critical IoT applications. The testbeds include SafeStep@AU (safestep.eng.auburn.edu), a live semantic-communication testbed for pedestrian safety monitoring at a signalized intersection in Auburn, AL. Outputs: [C2, P2].
  • Distributed Network Control in Dynamic Military Environments: A Learning-Based Approach — From Theory to Implementation
    Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Auburn University, Auburn, AL, USA (Jan. 2026 – Present) Role: postdoctoral researcher Sponsor: U.S. Army Research Office (ARO), cooperative agreement W911NF-24-2-0205. PI: Yin Sun (Auburn University). Co-PIs: Ness B. Shroff (The Ohio State University), Kaushik R. Chowdhury (The University of Texas at Austin). Learning-based distributed network control for dynamic environments, including distributed cross-layer optimization for covert multi-hop, multi-modal networks, developed with The Ohio State University and the DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory. Outputs: [C1, P1].
  • AQUASMARTT – Data Acquisition with Underwater and Above-Water Sustainable Wireless Nodes for Monitoring How Climatic Changes Affects Natural Disasters and Biodiversity
    Department of Engineering, University of Palermo, Palermo, Italy (Nov. 2022 – Dec. 2025) Role: Ph.D. researcher Sponsor: European Union – NextGenerationEU, Italian National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), Italian Ministry of University and Research (MUR) partnership PE00000001 “RESTART – Telecommunications of the Future”, Spoke 6 “Innovative Architectures and Extreme Environments”, focused project F16. PI: Michele Zorzi (University of Padova). Partners: University of Padova, University of Catania, Sapienza University of Rome. Research and development of intelligent networking components for coastal sensing and underwater acoustic networks: channel-quality prediction, adaptive modulation and power control, AoI-aware feedback scheduling, energy-efficient resource allocation, DESERT-based simulation and protocol-level evaluation for IoUT scenarios, and integration aspects of heterogeneous underwater sensing and communication systems. Outputs: [J4] (listed among the project's publications); [J2, C4] (acknowledging the RESTART programme).
  • 4Frailty – Smart Sensors, Infrastructures, and Management Models for the Safety of Frail Patients
    Department of Engineering, University of Palermo, Palermo, Italy (Jul. 2023 – Oct. 2023) Role: Ph.D. researcher Sponsor: MUR, National Operational Programme (PON) “Ricerca e Innovazione” 2014–2020 and Development and Cohesion Fund (FSC), project ARS01_00345 (“Technologies for Living Environments”). PI: Bruno Andò (University of Catania). Consortium: 21 partners led by Distretto Tecnologico Sicilia Micro e Nano Sistemi. IoT-based remote-care monitoring platform integrating wearable sensors, Android interfaces, MQTT-based data exchange, LoRa connectivity, and secure data storage.
  • Secure and Efficient Wireless Communications – Intelligent Communication Algorithms and Integrated System Development for Underwater Wireless Communication Environments
    Department of Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering, University of Catania, Catania, Italy (Sep. 2020 – May 2021) Role: research intern PI: Alfio Lombardo (University of Catania) Design, integration, and field testing of intelligent communication and monitoring architectures for underwater wireless environments: nearshore shallow-water marine trials of an integrated acoustic/LoRa IoUT system at La Playa, Catania (underwater acoustic modems, sensing devices, control boards, LoRa connectivity, remote monitoring interfaces; evaluation of transmission delay, packet delivery, and achievable bit rate for multimedia and control data); IoT-based sensing, MQTT-based data exchange, digital-twin modules, web dashboards, integrated sensing/communication prototypes, and simulated wake-up radio mechanisms. Outputs: [J6].
