Education

  • Ph.D. in Information and Communication Technologies
    Department of Engineering, University of Palermo, Palermo, Italy (Nov. 2022 – Dec. 2025) Degree awarded: 17 Dec. 2025 Thesis: “Learning and Adaptation for Efficient and Resilient Internet of Underwater Things Communications” Supervisor: Prof. Sergio Palazzo Three-part thesis on Internet of Underwater Things (IoUT) communications: (i) model-based channel prediction from real acoustic traces; (ii) lightweight multi-armed bandit frameworks for physical-layer-aware link adaptation (modulation and power control), distributed (multi-player) decision making, and Age of Information (AoI)-aware feedback scheduling, targeting energy-efficient resource allocation; (iii) bandit- and reinforcement-learning-based routing for energy balance and jamming resilience. Validated through network simulations under realistic channel, latency, and energy constraints.
  • M.Sc. in Telecommunications Engineering
    Department of Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering, University of Catania, Catania, Italy (Oct. 2019 – Oct. 2022) Degree awarded: 26 Oct. 2022 Thesis: “Characterization of Underwater Communication Channels in Shallow Water Environments: From On-Field Measurements to Markov-Based Models” Supervisor: Prof. Sergio Palazzo Co-supervisors: Prof. Laura Galluccio, Dr. Fabio Busacca Finite-state Markov models of shallow-water acoustic channels, derived from and validated against experimental traces of the LOON testbed of the NATO Centre for Maritime Research and Experimentation (CMRE): acoustic trace processing, channel impulse response analysis, SNR estimation, channel-quality assessment, propagation-induced variability, and MATLAB-based comparison of candidate models.
  • B.Sc. in Electronic Engineering
    Department of Electrical, Electronic and Computer Engineering, University of Catania, Catania, Italy (Sep. 2015 – Mar. 2020) Degree awarded: 20 Mar. 2020 Thesis: “Implementation of a Haptic Interface for Tactile Internet Scenarios in 5G Networks” Supervisor: Prof. Sergio Palazzo Co-supervisor: Prof. Giovanni Schembra Hapkit-based haptic interface with Arduino device control and a Mininet/SDN emulation environment; packet-level latency evaluation under different network-delay and routing conditions.
