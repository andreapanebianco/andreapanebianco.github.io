About Me

Andrea Panebianco received the Bachelor’s degree in Electronic Engineering in March 2019 and the Master’s degree in Telecommunication Engineering in October 2022, both from the University of Catania, Italy. He is currently a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Technologies at the University of Palermo, Italy. In 2023, he was awarded the Francesco Carassa Award for best presentation and publication. His research focuses on Underwater Acoustic Communications.

Research Interests

  • Underwater Acoustic Communications
  • Adaptive Modulation and Power Control
  • Age-of-Information–aware Feedback Scheduling
  • Multi-agent Learning Frameworks (MAB, DRL, Hierarchical Approaches)
